## Page body

**PHAGE** is a small microbial hunting game. You're a phage drifting in a petri dish. Steer by aiming the mouse and your cell orbits the cursor. Line your attack ring up with prey to devour it.

Each run you harvest nutrients. Between runs you spend them on a sprawling upgrade tree that reveals itself as you push deeper into the fog.

**Prey that fights back**
- **Oblivious** cells drift around, easy food.
- **Aware** cells notice you, give chase and slip away when cornered.
- **Ranged** cells back off, charge up and fire.
- **Armoured** cells soak up damage before cracking open.

**Controls**
- Mouse: aim (your cell orbits the cursor)
- Attack: automatic, so aim the ring at prey
- ESC: pause
- Space: start

Progress saves automatically in your browser.
